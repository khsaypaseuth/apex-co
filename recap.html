Project Recap

SV Consulting Website

A premium bilingual (English + Lao) website and Knowledge Center for a business, legal, immigration, accounting & tax consulting firm in Laos — a business unit of Super Vision Co., Ltd.

Overview

What was built

A complete, fully static, production-buildable website: 13 main pages, a Knowledge Center with 12 starter articles (9-category taxonomy, live search + filter), a Lao Laws Library with 8 plain-language law topics (8 categories, search + filter), 6 business guides with lead-capture forms, FAQ (5 sections), a CMS-ready News section with a designed empty state, and a contact page — everything in both English and Lao (85 prerendered pages), with Thai / Vietnamese / Chinese architecture prepared but inactive.

Every legal, tax, and immigration statement is deliberately hedged and carries machine-checkable Needs legal verification markers until a professional review — nothing unverified is presented as fact, and the site-wide legal disclaimer renders on every relevant page in both languages.

Quick start

How to run locally

npm install
npm run dev        # development → http://localhost:3000
npm run build      # production build (validates all content)
npm run start      # serve the production build

Visiting / redirects to /en (or /lo if the browser prefers Lao). Requires Node 22+. No environment variables, no database, no external services.

Stack: Next.js 16.2.10 (App Router) · TypeScript strict · Tailwind CSS v4 (CSS-first) · Markdown via gray-matter + unified · Zod-validated frontmatter.

Design

Design direction & brand system

“Modern editorial corporate luxury for Southeast Asian professional services.” Calm, premium, trustworthy: generous whitespace, thin gold rules, serif display over sans body, subtle motion only (hover states, soft reveals, reduced-motion respected).

Deep Navy#0A1B2A — dark surfaces, footer, hero
Royal Navy#203D63 — links, accents
Warm Gold#C8A53A — sparing CTA accent, rules
Ivory#F8F0DC — light section backgrounds
Slate#6B7280 — muted text, borders

Typography: Fraunces (English display) + Manrope (English body); Noto Serif Lao (Lao display) + Noto Sans Lao (Lao body) — the font stack switches per locale, and Lao body text gets a raised 1.8 line-height. (This recap uses system fonts so it works offline.)

Imagery: 19 license-safe Pexels photos (muted, warm-neutral grade), stored locally with a full attribution log; alt text never claims a location that isn’t confirmed.

Content

Knowledge Center & Lao Laws Library

Knowledge Center — 12 starter articles, 9 categories

Categories: Starting a Business · Tax & Accounting · Visa & Immigration · Lao Law Basics · Labour & Employment · Investment · Marriage & Family · Living in Laos · Compliance Checklists. Each article: title, summary, last-updated, reading time, category, verification status, disclaimer, related services + articles, CTA. Client-side search + category filter.

Lao Laws Library — 8 starter topics, 8 categories

Plain-language law summaries (never full legal texts): summary, who it affects, key obligations, common mistakes, related links, verification badge, sources box where a real official portal is known. Categories: Company · Investment · Tax · Labour · Immigration · Family · Intellectual Property · Commercial Contracts. Search + category filter.

Content lives as markdown with Zod-validated frontmatter in content/en/ and content/lo/ (identical slugs; only title/summary translate). Invalid frontmatter fails the build with a descriptive error.

Reach

SEO strategy

  • Every page: templated title, description, canonical, Open Graph (branded 1200×630 image), via one shared metadata builder.
  • hreflang alternates for active locales only (en, lo, x-default → en) — parked languages can’t cause thin-content penalties.
  • Generated sitemap.xml (78 URLs with per-entry hreflang) and robots.txt, derived from the same loaders as the routes — they cannot drift.
  • All 15 master-plan target keywords mapped one-primary-page-each with supporting content clusters (docs/seo-map.md).
  • 85/85 pages statically prerendered; semantic HTML, clean heading order.
Trust

Legal disclaimer strategy

  • One canonical disclaimer (exact master-plan wording) rendered on every service, article, law, guide, and FAQ page — in both languages.
  • Every factual file carries a verificationStatus; nothing ships as “verified” — 24 files per locale are marked needs-verification, with the literal marker line at every unverified claim cluster (Lao files carry English + Lao markers, 45 each, in exact parity).
  • Zero unhedged specifics: a QA regex scan found no fees, rates, or processing times stated as fact anywhere.
  • Only two confidently-real official portals are cited as sources; authorities are otherwise named in prose, never guess-linked.

Honesty

Known limitations

  • Native Lao review pending — all Lao content is machine-drafted at professional register; review required before publication.
  • Legal verification pending — 24 content files per locale are marked and await professional review before statuses flip to “verified”.
  • Placeholders — phone, email, WhatsApp/LINE links, domain (svconsulting.la), and both form backends are clearly-marked placeholders (checklist in README.md).
  • Thai / Vietnamese / Chinese deferred — routes, types, and folders prepared; content intentionally postponed until EN + LO are finalized.
  • Minor: OG image uses a serif stand-in font; one documented AA contrast exception (gold eyebrow labels) awaits an owner decision.
Next

Future roadmap

  1. Pre-launch: replace placeholders (contacts, domain), native Lao review, legal verification pass with real sources, wire contact + lead forms to a real backend or email service.
  2. Activate remaining languages: Thai → Vietnamese → Chinese (one-switch activation, documented in README and content/th/README.md).
  3. News: connect the CMS-ready News structure to a real content source and publish legal/tax/visa updates.
  4. Grow the libraries: more articles (labour, investment categories are thinnest), commercial-contracts law topic, per-page OG images, URL-persistent filters.
